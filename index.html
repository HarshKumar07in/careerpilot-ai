CareerPilot AI is an autonomous career and opportunity agent designed to help students discover relevant internships, verify opportunities, and move from a career goal to concrete application action.

Explore the idea ↓

What we’re building

A smarter way for students to navigate the overwhelming internship and job search process.

Discover

Find opportunities aligned with a student's skills, interests, goals, and career direction.

Verify & Match

Analyze opportunities and compare them with a student's profile to surface the most relevant options.

Take Action

Turn recommendations into practical next steps and personalized application support.

The problem

Students often search across dozens of platforms, face irrelevant listings, struggle to judge which opportunities are worth pursuing, and lose time figuring out what to do next.

Our vision

Career discovery should feel less like endless searching and more like having an intelligent career co-pilot that understands your goals and helps you act on them.
